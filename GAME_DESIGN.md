# Winding World

*Trace the loop. Deform the lie. Lash what χ cannot explain.*

Self-contained game world — no Arcade of Alexandria fiction inside the game.

## Story
The Winding World is a realm of mutable spaces: corridors that rewrite themselves, surfaces that sprout teeth, and enemies whose defenses are the shape of the ground they stand on. **Gyroid** takes up the Fundamental Lash with Seifert (route coach) and Chi Euler (cell-counting automaton). Their quarry is the Space-Born: Nullhomotoe the Sphere, Tori Dual-Circuit, Genus Gerry, Covert Sheet, and later preview foes Boundary Barry, Cup Productrix, and Hop Off. The campaign runs from deformation through π₁ and coverings, then optional homology / cohomology / higher-homotopy systems — as whip moves, not trivia.

## Goal
Win encounters by constructing and reasoning about loops, fixed-endpoint homotopies, concatenation, reduced words, and lifted paths. Euler characteristic is a structural clue only — never the attack button.
